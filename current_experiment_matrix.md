## Notes

- `capacity_fair=yes` means total parameter count is kept effectively aligned with the non-moe baseline; `active_path_fair` rows may still have larger total parameter count.
- `PPL@64`: for rows refreshed on or after `2026-05-25`, loaded-checkpoint evaluation with `batch_size=4`, `max_samples=256`.
- `PPL@1024`: loaded-checkpoint evaluation with `batch_size=4`, `max_samples=1024`.

## Summary

- **copy_noise_4e_full_top1_20k_takeaway**: training from scratch to 20k substantially improves over the 5k top-1 variant and now very slightly beats the non-moe baseline on both 64-batch and 1024-sample evaluation, but expert similarity remains very high.
- **complement_pair_6e_half_top2_20k_takeaway**: the complement-pair design continues to improve with longer training and now clearly beats the non-moe baseline on both 64-batch and 1024-sample evaluation while preserving moderate specialization and healthy ternary statistics.
- **complement_pair_6e_half_top2_aux0001_20k_takeaway**: lowering router aux to 0.0001 and training from scratch to 20k yields the strongest complement-pair result so far, improving over the earlier aux=0.005 20k run without harming load balance.
- **complement_pair_6e_half_top2_aux0001_layers6_23_5k_takeaway**: expanding MoE coverage to layers 6-23 increases parameter count but does not outperform the standard layer12-23 5k configuration, so depth expansion looks lower priority than staying on the 12-layer template.
- **relaxed_vs_pairfree_5k_takeaway**: among the two aggressive routing ablations, relaxed top-2 with coverage penalty fails to unlock new useful paths, while pair-plus-free top-3 is the only one that produces a clear 5K gain; that makes extra active compute look more promising than simply relaxing the pair constraint.
- **continued_finetune_10k_priority**: if continuation-style follow-up is pursued, partial full fine-tune ranks above local token-mixer+norm, but both gains are too small to justify treating continuation as a new mainline over the scratch 20K complement-pair baseline.
- **random_extreme_local_backbone_40k_takeaway**: Both random-extreme local-backbone 40K runs achieve near-zero expert similarity but formal PPL collapses badly, so forcing extreme specialization in this way is counterproductive.
- **pair_plus_free_scale050_20k_takeaway**: Extending pair-plus-free with free_expert_scale=0.50 to 20K keeps routing healthy and improves over 5K variants, but it still does not beat the best strict 6E complement scratch-20K run.
- **shared_residual_isoarea_5k_takeaway**: Both strict-parameter-fair shared-residual iso-area 5K runs are stable and fully evaluable, but neither is competitive: alpha=0.25 is slightly better than alpha=0.10, yet both remain far worse than the non-MoE baseline and current complement-pair mainline.
- **full_shared_bugfixed_5k_takeaway**: With the shared-copy bug fixed and identity verified, exact full-shared 2816 controls return to dense-baseline quality. The 8x32 top2 variants are slightly best, and local_backbone_ft adds only a marginal benefit over moe_only.
- **shared_residual_topchannel_bugfixed_5k_takeaway**: Clean reruns of the 2288 topchannel + discarded residual family remain decisively bad: all four land around 20.06-20.12 PPL@1024. 8x64 top2 is slightly better on PPL than 4x128 top1, but with much worse routing imbalance, so this route is not viable.
- **shared_only_from_dense_2288_diagnostic_takeaway**: The shared-only-from-dense diagnostic at width 2288 is even worse than the trained shared_residual checkpoints by roughly 0.43-0.49 PPL, so sparse residuals are partially recovering lost quality. The primary damage comes from compressing the shared path to 2288, not from adding sparse residuals per se.
